# Product Guidelines

## Prose and Communication Style
- **Professional & Academic:** The tone is clear, objective, and precise, tailored for scientific researchers.
- **Modern & Minimalist:** Communication is concise and direct, prioritizing information density over fluff.

## Visual Identity & Aesthetic
- **Holographic & Futuristic:** The UI employs subtle glowing effects, transparency, and a high-tech aesthetic, utilizing components like holographic borders and animated backgrounds to create a sense of advanced technology.

## User Experience (UX) Principles
- **Deep Focus:** The design minimizes distractions, ensuring that the primary research task remains the central focus.
- **Context-Awareness:** The application provides relevant tools and options dynamically based on the user's current search mode or media analysis task.

## Brand Messaging & Tone
- **Authority & Trust:** Messaging emphasizes grounded citations and scientific accuracy to build user confidence in the research results.
- **Accessibility & Clarity:** Complex findings are presented with clarity, ensuring that deep research insights remain accessible to the user.
