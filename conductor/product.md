# Initial Concept

An AI-powered research assistant helping scientists find tools, models, and data with aesthetic precision and grounded references.

# Product Definition

## Target Audience
- Academic Researchers
- Data Scientists
- R&D Engineers

## Core Goals
- Help scientists find tools, models, and data with aesthetic precision.
- Provide grounded references and high-quality citations.
- Optimize productivity through advanced visualization and multimodal interaction.

## High-Level Features
- **Intelligent Search:** Grounded search for scientific tools, models, and datasets.
- **Aesthetic UI:** A precise, customizable interface designed for deep focus and productivity.
- **Multimodal Research:** Support for voice-to-research, media analysis, and geographic search (Maps).
- **Deep Research:** Advanced analysis mode for complex, multi-step queries.
- **Session Management:** Local storage of search history and research sessions.
- **Collaboration & Export:** Real-time insight sharing and multi-format export (Markdown, PDF).

## Value Proposition
Lumina Research bridges the gap between raw data search and specialized scientific research, providing a highly aesthetic, multimodal interface that prioritizes grounded accuracy and user productivity.

## Success Metrics
- Accuracy and relevance of research results and citations.
- User engagement and time saved during the research process.
- High user satisfaction with the aesthetic and functional UI.
